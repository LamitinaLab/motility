# Motility Analysis Output Guide

“Current output” means actively regenerated by the present workflow. “Legacy” means retained from an earlier analysis or presentation workflow; it does not imply scientific invalidity.

## `Data/Processed/`

### Core production and analysis inputs

- `track_metrics.csv`: worm-level production speed and body-angular-rate summaries.
- `normalized_tracks.csv`: normalized Position trajectories and interval measurements.
- `thrashing_data.csv`: canonical worm-level thrashing estimates and eligibility/QC context.
- `thrashing_segments.csv`: eligible continuous-segment frequency diagnostics.
- `fit_data.csv`: frame-level Fit values.
- `length_data.csv`: frame-level length input/QC context.
- `body_angle_data.csv`: midpoint bending-angle frame data.
- `body_angles_multiple_data.csv`: 11-position Multiple-Angle validation signals.
- `center_points_data.csv`: 17-point geometry for the preferred wave analysis.

### Ingestion and alignment QC

- `raw_export_duplicate_audit.csv`
- `raw_source_alignment_audit.csv`
- `raw_sampling_density_audit.csv`

These describe source discovery, duplicate handling, alignment, and sampling; they are not biological phenotypes.

## `results/qc/`

Major output families include:

- `body_wave_*`: Multiple-Angle precursor, segment validity, cycle support, and examples.
- `center_point_wave_*`: Center-Point segment/track summaries, validation, wave properties, amplitude, and duty.
- `center_vs_multiple_*`: representation agreement.
- `center_point_local_bending_kymograph.*`: local-turning-angle wave visualization.
- `rk212_wave_*`: deterministic frequency sanity checks.
- `translation_body_*` and `center_point_wave_efficiency_*`: translation/body-wave and U/c diagnostics.
- `seven_primary_phenotype_*`: correlation, redundancy, and multicollinearity.
- `primary_pca_eligibility_*`: complete-case retention and eligibility reasons.
- `multivariate_motility_pca_*`: PCA inputs, scores, loadings, variance, centroids, readable plots, and sensitivities.
- `video_cluster_*` and `leave_one_video_out_*`: acquisition-cluster context and robustness.
- `recording_duration_sensitivity_*`: duration, window-position, eligibility, agreement, and PCA stability.
- `sample_size_*`: standardized planning, eligibility/clustering costs, raw-unit MDEs, and biological targets.
- `sample_size_control_anchored_*`: N2-control heteroscedastic variability, MDE, required tracks, bounded simulations, and RK212 context.

Diagnostic files do not redefine production measurements. Candidate wave phenotypes remain eligibility-aware, and missing values remain `NaN`.

## `results/prism/`

- Seven phenotype-specific wide CSVs contain one genotype per column.
- Each finite value is an individual worm, not a genotype average.
- Eligibility-related missingness is preserved as blank/`NaN`.
- `seven_primary_motility_phenotypes_master.csv` retains identifiers, phenotype values, and eligibility/context fields.

Use the master table to audit values selected from a wide Prism file.

## Presentation outputs

- `results/figures/mean_speed_comparison.*`: raw and N2-normalized speed.
- `results/figures/thrashing_frequency_comparison.*`: canonical frequencies and coverage.
- `results/figures/summary_figure.*`: retained speed/thrashing overview.
- `results/qc/seven_motility_phenotypes_and_pca_summary.*`: seven phenotypes plus PCA.
- `results/qc/multivariate_motility_pca_scores_readable.*`: readable 2D PCA.
- `results/qc/multivariate_motility_pca_genotype_summary.*`: genotype-centroid context.
- `results/qc/multivariate_motility_pca_scores_3d.*`: static 3D PCA.
- `results/qc/multivariate_motility_pca_scores_3d_interactive.html`: optional Plotly output.
- `results/qc/sample_size_raw_mde_vs_tracks.*`: raw-unit MDE planning.
- `results/qc/sample_size_control_anchored_mde.*` and `sample_size_n2_speed_planning.*`: control-anchored planning.

Consult associated CSVs for numerical reporting and traceability.

## Legacy and retained compatibility outputs

- `results/plot_csv/`: established plotting exports.
- `results/plot_csv_prism/`: older GraphPad-oriented compatibility tables.
- Older figures under `results/` and `results/figures/` may support prior presentations or provenance.
- Multiple-Angle wave outputs remain useful validation history although Center Points are preferred geometrically.

Do not delete a legacy file without tracing its generating cell and downstream use.

## Eligibility reminders

Speed and median body angular rate have broad eligibility. Frequency, wavelength, phase-linearity R², and amplitude require a valid coherent wave. Conditional duty requires qualifying continuous time; it may equal 0 when enough data exist but no wave is detected. Insufficient data produce `NaN`.

Videos are acquisition clusters, not biological replicates. Current results represent one experimental run.
